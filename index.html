Quick guide

How to play

  1. 1Drag left or right on the screen, or use the arrow keys (or A and D), to move your cannon.
  2. 2Auto fire is on, so the cannon shoots by itself. Tap or hold the screen, or press Space, to shoot harder.
  3. 3Destroy enemies before they reach you. Each one that gets through costs a life.
  4. 4Kill enemies in a row to raise your score multiplier, up to x5.
  5. 5Grab the glowing crystals for triple shot, rapid fire, a shield or an extra life.
