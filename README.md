# Canon Armed

Stop the alien drones before they reach your cannon. Three levels: Normal, Medium and Hard.
The screen uses only three colours (primary, secondary and text) on black (dark theme) or white (light theme), following your system setting.

## Run on Windows (Command Prompt)
1. Unzip the folder and open CMD inside `canon-armed`.
2. Run: python -m http.server 8000
3. Open http://localhost:8000 in Chrome or Edge (internet is needed for the 3D and style libraries).

## How to play
- Move: drag left or right on the screen, arrow keys or A / D.
- Fire: Auto fire is on by default (press F or tap the Auto fire button to toggle). Tap or hold the screen, or press Space, to shoot harder.
- Pause: P or Esc (or the pause button). Sound: M.
- Start / play again: Enter.

## Levels
- Normal: 3 lives. 20 enemies per wave, rising to 30.
- Medium: 2 lives. 20 to 30 enemies per wave, faster.
- Hard: 1 life. 20 to 30 enemies per wave, fastest. One hit and you are out.

Your best score is saved separately for each level in this browser.
